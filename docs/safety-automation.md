# Safety and time automation

These newly written domains share the platform transaction, guild lock, idempotency ledger, outbox and fresh actor policy. Every path below begins `/guilds/:guildId`. Browser input cannot submit a Gateway observation. All records and evidence are scoped to their guild.

## Safety (M4)

`GET/POST /forms`, `PUT /forms/:id`, `POST /forms/:id/publish` and `POST /forms/:id/submissions` provide a draft/publish/application journey. Creation/editing accepts `{form: FormSchema}` (editing also needs `expectedRevision`). A published form has immutable numbered versions. Each application stores its full submitted version and answers; later changes do not change that application. `POST /applications/:id/resubmit` lets the original submitter revise a changes-requested application against its original form version. Required/blocked roles and repeat limits are checked against the current actor. Unknown answer fields, missing answers, oversized values and values outside declared choices are rejected.

The shared `prepareApplicationForm` helper returns the current published immutable snapshot and version after checking tenant, enabled feature, current roles and submission limits. `submitApplication` applies the same rules and answer validation to both browser submissions and persisted Discord intake. An unfinished flow is bound to its published version: drafting or republishing the form requires the member to reopen the current form. Previously submitted applications retain their original snapshot. Caller-supplied ownership is never accepted; submission ownership comes from the fresh actor, and the platform mutation identity prevents duplicate applications on replay.

`GET /applications` shows members their submissions and `forms.review` staff their queue. `POST /applications/:id/review` accepts `expectedRevision`, `decision` (`advance`, `accept`, `reject`, `request_changes`) and a reason. Review stages cannot be skipped for acceptance. Staff cannot review their own application. Final decisions are immutable. Accepted optional role delivery and notifications use the durable outbox; review capability does not grant general server configuration access.

`GET/POST /cases` and `POST /cases/:id/notes` support numbered warns, notes, timeouts, kicks, bans/unbans, scoped purge, slowmode and locks/unlocks. Target/channel requirements, durations, scope and explicit confirmation are validated. Warnings and Discord mutations remain **pending** until a worker confirms the provider result. A case notification failure does not reverse a successful punishment. Moderation workers must fetch the current actor and bot authority, owner/target hierarchy and destination permissions at execution. Punishment expiry must identify its original instance and never remove a newer timeout or overwrite a newer lock.

`GET /cases/mine` lists the member's own cases (no private notes or staff notes) with whether each can still be appealed. `GET/POST /reports` and `/appeals` provide separate private intake. `POST /reports/:id/decision` and `/appeals/:id/decision` require distinct reviewer capabilities, a revision, decision and reason. Members see their own intake; reviewers see the permitted queue. Appeals can reference only the submitter's applied case, one appeal per case/member. Accepting an appeal records the decision; a reversal requires an independently authorized moderation action.

`POST /verification/challenge` and `/verification/verify` implement rules acknowledgement and optional arithmetic challenges. Account age derives from a valid Discord ID, and member age requires an actual observed join time. Challenges expire after five minutes, allow at most five attempted answers, and are bound to actor/rules version. Wrong answers commit an attempt counter and a clear `verified: false` response instead of granting a role. Role grants are pending effects and require current safe hierarchy checks. Verification is a configured gate and does not promise bot-proof identity.

`POST /safety/test` evaluates the same bounded literal keyword, mention, link/invite, attachment, capitalization, message burst and duplicate filter engine used by Gateway ingestion. The response is marked `simulation` and has zero effects. Arbitrary regular expressions are deliberately unsupported. Normalization preserves multilingual input; uncased languages do not count as uppercase.

R27 observes by default. Enforcement needs an enabled deterministic rule, fresh non-bot source, exemption checks, per-rule cooldown and guild action budget. Late messages cannot discard newer observations from the burst/duplicate window. R28 observes actual join windows and can queue a configured restricted quarantine role, with bounded actions; trusted sources are excluded before counting signals. Delayed prior joins cannot overwrite tenure from a later rejoin. R29 records destructive-event evidence and confirmed/ambiguous/unknown attribution and alerts staff. **Anti-nuke automatic bans and faithful resource restoration are unsupported**: unknown attribution never authorizes a punishment, and deleted content cannot be invented. `GET /safety/incidents` and `POST /safety/incidents/:id/recover` retain an auditable manual recovery review.

### Ticket staff workflow (R23)

Routes under `/tickets`: `POST /:id/priority` (`low`, `normal`, `high`, `urgent`), `POST /:id/assign` (only to staff whose availability is `available`), `POST /:id/waiting` (`awaiting_member` or back to `claimed`), `GET /staff`, `PUT /staff/me` (`available`, `away`, `off` with a note) and `GET /metrics` (30-day window with stated definitions). All require ticket staff of the current configuration (or the ticket's snapshot for per-ticket actions) and a matching revision. Ticket settings `workflow.reminderMinutes` / `escalationMinutes` / `escalationRoleId` schedule `ticket.sla` outbox timers from the creation snapshot. The worker skips claimed, closed or already-nudged tickets, posts through a tracked send (one message per timer) with role mentions limited to the configured roles, and escalation raises priority to at least `high`.

## Time and automation (M5)

- `GET/POST /polls`, `POST /polls/:id/votes` and `/close`: single/multiple selection, current role eligibility, immutable deadlines, vote-change policy and result visibility. Results are aggregate; members also receive their own ballot. Votes retain Discord member IDs, so the product must not claim anonymity. Optional Discord publication is a valid Components V2 select using persistent `vote.cast` routing.
- `GET/POST /events`, `PUT /events/:id`, `/rsvp` and `/cancel`: UTC instants, IANA display timezone, role eligibility, serialized capacity/waitlist claims, oldest-waiting promotion, reminders and cancellation. RSVP accepts `{choice:"attending"}` or `{choice:"declined"}`. Event edits invalidate old timers and already queued reminder messages and reschedule them. Capacity cannot shrink below confirmed attendance. RSVP closes at the start, and reminders cannot deliver after the start. Cancellation notices are specifically marked so ordinary queued reminders cannot deliver afterward.
- `GET/POST /reminders`, `/reminders/:id/cancel`: actor-owned personal DM destinations or explicitly authorized server channels, active-count caps, durable once/daily/weekly recurrence and misfire policy. Channel permissions/DM refusal remain provider delivery results. Cancellation is checked immediately before dispatch.
- `GET/PUT/DELETE /birthday`: optional month/day, IANA timezone, private/public sharing and explicit February 29 policy. No birth year/age is collected. Public announcement/role actions require public opt-in; private birthdays get private notifications. Edits/privacy changes fence pending notifications and grants; deleting the birthday stops queued future messages. Changing/removing a birthday cancels undelivered grants and queues removal for an applied owned role. Timed removal requires the original assignment instance and remains eligible if the module is subsequently disabled.
- `GET /voice-rooms`, `/voice-rooms/:id/control`: join-to-create rooms owned by the initiating member; owner controls rename/limit/lock/unlock/transfer/permit/revoke/delete. A member cannot target another room/channel. Empty-room cleanup rechecks actual Discord occupants immediately before deletion; stale observed occupancy alone never authorizes removal.
- `GET /invites`: persisted confirmed/ambiguous/unknown attribution with before/after observations. Attribution is confirmed only for a single stable invite whose use increased exactly once, with one observed join, complete snapshots and unchanged inviter identity. Vanity changes, vanished invites, newly observed already-used invites, simultaneous joins or missing permissions remain uncertain. Optional R41 rewards require observed current presence, tenure/account age, a once-per-member/inviter reward identity and role safety. Leave reversal includes applied rewards and cancels queued grants. A queued removal rechecks other earned instances before dispatch, so it cannot remove a role still justified by another reward.
- `GET/POST /schedules`, `/schedules/:id/cancel`: immutable public template-version snapshots, destination, timezone, durable occurrence identity, once/daily/weekly recurrence, skip or send-once misfires and run history. Private templates cannot become channel automation.
- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /replies`: literal exact/contains/prefix matches, excluded roles/channels, per-channel cooldown, at most three replies per source. Bot/webhook/generated messages cannot cause loops.
- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /sticky`: one sticky owner per channel, immutable public template version, debounced updates, current message ownership and replacement delivery tracking. The bot's own messages never trigger repost.
- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /workflows`, `/workflows/preview`, `/workflows/:id/execute`, `GET /workflow-runs` and `/workflow-runs/:id/cancel`: typed messages/roles/tickets, forward-only branches and bounded waits. Immutable definition snapshots, action/depth/deadline budgets and step records prevent loops and replay of successful earlier steps. A preview has zero effects; the next effect is queued only after the preceding delivery is settled. The provider worker refreshes initiating member eligibility and destination/role policy for every delayed action.
- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /custom-commands`: only `spark-` namespaced declarative workflow commands, role gates and a 20-command guild limit. Registration is a durable diff, not wholesale deletion of all commands. Usage is persisted by interaction identity. Missing registration or permission is shown as pending/failed rather than active success.

Recurring local times use IANA timezone conversion. The first instant of a repeated DST time is selected once; nonexistent local minutes move forward to the first valid minute. Missed runs more than five minutes overdue are skipped under `skip`; `send_once` emits one catch-up message and schedules the next future occurrence, avoiding a flood of historical sends. UTC is persisted and timezone labels are retained.

## Worker contract

`ingestSafetyEvent` and `ingestAutomationEvent` are Gateway-only domain functions. Domain ingestion validates the event schema, stores an event identity and never performs network I/O inside its transaction. `processSafetyDue(ctx, 'safety.timeout.expire', payload)` checks the active `safety.punishment` instance before queuing `safety.timeout.clear`; the worker also compares a fresh Discord timeout timestamp. An old case or manually replaced timeout never authorizes removal of the new instance.

`processAutomationDue(ctx, 'automation.due', {kind,id,revision,dueAt?})` validates the live generation and scheduled instant before committing a durable run. Newly queued timers include `dueAt`; earlier checkpoint jobs derive the instant from their source record. An early timer returns a retryable error and consumes no run or business operation. Every timer enters through the platform transaction/guild lock. `guardAutomationEffect(ctx,payload)` must run immediately before external dispatch, followed by current Discord permissions/resource checks. `completeAutomationEffect(ctx,payload,result)` records settled workflow steps and queues exactly the next step; it also updates sticky ownership after a successful post. No delivery handler may mark Discord state applied before receiving success.

Effects: `safety.moderate`, `safety.notify`, `safety.protection`, `safety.verification.role`, `automation.due`, `automation.send`, `automation.role`, `automation.voice`, `automation.ticket`, `automation.custom.register`. Provider mutations use narrow handlers; an unknown effect type fails visibly.

## Verification evidence

On 2026-10-11, the focused community/safety-time unit suites passed 43 cases, including 23 safety/time tests. They cover filter safety/multilingual behavior, late burst observations, trusted signal exclusions, rejoin tenure, self-review denial, pending cases, verification attempt limits, budgets/loops, DST folds/gaps, leap-day policy, invite evidence, role ballots, reminder ownership/cancellation/misfires, birthday privacy revocation, workflow sequencing and voice ownership. The focused PostgreSQL suites passed 25 cases, including 14 safety/time integration tests against local disposable PostgreSQL 17.6. These exercise serialized RSVP/review races, private intake, immutable versions, narrow reviewer grants, early timer rollback and later execution, queued event/birthday invalidation, applied invite reversal, observed-only safety, step cancellation, UTC timers and superseded punishment instances. These checks do not claim a live Discord permission, OAuth, DM, channel creation, registration or timer delivery check.

Live checks need a configured Discord application/token, target test guild, permitted staff/member accounts, message-content/member intents where features require them, and the relevant bot channel/role/audit permissions. Fresh hierarchy denial, obsolete expiry, restart recovery, DM denial, ambiguous channel-create reconciliation and real gateway invite windows must be verified in that guild before release claims.
