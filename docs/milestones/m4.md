# M4 — Safety and support expansion

Complete locally. Delivered through the M4 milestone PR (see progress.md for the PR and CI run). Live Discord acceptance remains an external prerequisite.

M4 continues the paused work from `claude/amazing-maxwell-yonuhl` (`cafe717`: timed bans and locks, the moderation and forms workspaces, typed safety contracts). That work was merged onto the UI/UX pass, so every M4 page uses the shared design system.

## Delivered behavior

**R26 moderation**

- Numbered cases cover:
  - warns, private notes and timeouts
  - kicks, bans and unbans
  - scoped purge and slowmode
  - locks and unlocks
- Bans and locks can be timed. Expiry removes only the punishment instance it belongs to: a newer timeout or a manual change is never undone.
- Cases show the real Discord delivery outcome.
- Case DMs now include the case number and say how to appeal.

**R25 forms and applications**

- The builder publishes versioned forms. Each application keeps the exact version it was submitted against.
- Review is staged, with reasons; reviewers cannot review their own application.
- The forms tab says when the module is off and links to its settings.
- A reopened draft can now be published. Previously the unset applicant-role field made the editor look edited, so Publish stayed disabled.

**R30 reports and appeals**

- Separate staff queues; each decision is recorded with a reason that the member receives.
- Evidence links open as `noopener noreferrer nofollow`.
- Members send private reports, and appeal their own applied cases once each, from the member profile.
- A new member route, `GET /cases/mine`, lists the member's own cases for appeals. Private notes and staff notes stay hidden.

**R18 verification**

- Settings for the rules text and rules version, the verified role (only roles Sparko can grant are selectable), account age, time in the server, and an arithmetic challenge.
- The page states that verification grants a role and does not hide channels by itself.
- Members verify from their profile. A rules change forces the member to accept the new version.

**R27 auto moderation**

- Basic rule fields are shown up front; timing, exemptions and the rule identifier sit behind a disclosure.
- Mode and alert channel, the quarantine role, and an actions-per-minute budget.
- A rule tester runs the server's own rule engine. It shows which condition matched and what the bot would do, and enforces nothing.
- An incidents tab.

**R28 raid guard**

- A join-burst threshold and window, trusted roles, and observe or quarantine mode with the restricted role.
- Incidents.

**R29 anti-nuke**

- A threshold and window for destructive changes, observe or alert mode, and trusted members.
- The page states that this is detection, not prevention.
- Incidents show:
  - attribution as confirmed, ambiguous or unknown
  - what cannot be restored
  - the recorded manual recovery

**R21, R23, R24 tickets and staff workflow**

- Migration `202610110006_ticket_staff_workflow` adds:
  - ticket priority (checked low, normal, high or urgent)
  - the first-claim time
  - reminder and escalation markers
- Ticket settings gain reminder and escalation timers and an optional escalation role.
  - Timers are scheduled from the creation snapshot, so later setting changes do not affect existing tickets.
  - Timers stop once staff claim the ticket.
  - The worker posts each nudge exactly once through a tracked send. Escalation raises the ticket to high priority.
- Staff mark themselves available, away or off duty.
  - Tickets can be assigned or transferred only to available staff.
  - Staff set priority, mark a claimed ticket as waiting for the member and resume it, and add or remove participants.
- Staff metrics:
  - open tickets and tickets waiting for staff
  - escalations
  - median time to first claim and median time to resolve, each with its stated definition
  - per-staff claimed and closed counts
- Members cannot read staff availability or metrics.
- The inbox sorts by priority and shows a support-team panel. The ticket page shows staff actions and timer history.
- Transcripts can be read and searched in the page, through the same authorized API as the download.
  - Attachments show their name and size only, never a link that would expose a private file.
  - Partial captures say so.

## Setup

Apply the migration chain, now six migrations, to an explicitly disposable database following `docs/setup.md`. No new environment settings are needed. Reminder and escalation messages mention roles; Discord delivers those mentions only if the roles are mentionable or the bot has the Mention Everyone permission. Sparko never adds that permission itself.

## Manual smoke

1. In ticket settings, set a 5-minute reminder and a 10-minute escalation. Open a ticket in Discord and wait without claiming it. Confirm one reminder, then one escalation, and that the ticket becomes high priority.
2. Mark yourself available in the inbox. Assign the ticket to yourself, set it to urgent, and mark it as waiting for the member.
3. Turn on auto moderation in observe mode. Add an invite rule and test it with `discord.gg/test`. Post an invite in Discord and confirm that an observed incident appears and nothing was punished.
4. Turn on reports and appeals. As a member, send a report. As a different staff member, record a decision.
5. Warn a test member. As that member, appeal from the member profile.

## Remaining boundaries

The following still need a live Discord application and test guild:

- Gateway message, join and destructive events.
- Audit-log attribution.
- DMs.
- Role mentions in reminders.
- Quarantine role grants.

Anti-nuke never bans automatically and cannot restore deleted resources. Staff record manual recovery instead.

The waiting-for-member state is set by staff: Sparko does not yet move a ticket back automatically when the member replies.

Staff availability is self-reported. It does not track Discord presence.
