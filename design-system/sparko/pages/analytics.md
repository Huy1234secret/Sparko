# Analytics (overrides)

- Unobserved hours are gaps, never zero; observed quiet hours are zero. Partly observed hours are marked.
- Always show coverage ("N of M hours observed") beside totals, and units on every metric (messages, members, participant-minutes).
- Every chart has a text alternative (`aria-label`) and a per-day table; CSV export includes gaps and their reasons.
- Empty data says why (collection off, intent missing) and how to fix it, with a link to collection settings.
